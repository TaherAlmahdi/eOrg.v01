Nay more, no unhaissed Englishman, sitting in judgemennt on the events which passed in the interval between the 9th February and the 23rd June, can deny that the name of Siraj'd-dualah stands higher in the Scale of honor than does the name of Clive. He was the only one of the principal actors in that tragic. Arama who did not attempt to deceive!”—Col Malleson</p>
